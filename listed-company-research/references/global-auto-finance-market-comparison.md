- Carvana/CarMax loan pool delinquency rates 2-3x higher than benchmarks (2024 vintage loans)

- Long-term loans (84+ months) creating negative equity risk

## UK Market Specifics

- **PCP (Personal Contract Purchase) dominant**: >50% of new car finance
- **FCA strict regulation**:
  - 2021: Banned DCA (Discretionary Commission Arrangements)
  - 2024 Oct: Court ruling supports consumer claims
  - 2025 Mar: Announced potential £9B redress scheme (Source: FCA PS24/18)
- 2023 auto finance sales: new cars ~680K, used cars ~1.3M, subprime ~70K (Source: FCA Lender Survey)

## Japan Market Specifics

**Unique model: Residual-value loans (残価設定ローン)**
- Loan amount based on vehicle residual value rather than full price
- End-of-term options: repay, trade-in, or purchase
- Similar to European PCP but more flexible
- High lease penetration: corporate leases dominate
- Mature used car market: Gulliver, IDOM large chains

## Lessons for Chinese Fintech Platform Companies

### Funding Cost Structure
| Institution Type | Funding Cost Range | Notes |
|-----------------|-------------------|-------|
| **Banks** | 2.0-2.5% | Deposit base + low reserve cost, structural advantage |
| **AAA-rated leasing companies** | 2.3-2.5% | Bond issuance rate 2.37% (2024 weighted average), close to banks |
| **AA+ rated leasing companies** | 2.8-3.5% | Significant credit spread |
| **Small/medium leasing companies** | 4-6%+ | Limited funding channels, significantly higher than banks |

**Key insight from United Ratings (2024):** "In the low-interest-rate environment, banks' funding cost advantage is increasingly obvious — they not only compete with leasing companies for high-quality clients using low-rate loans."

### Strategic Implications
1. **Funding structure optimization**: Learn from Carvana's diversified funding sources (ABS, bank credit lines, institutional capital)
2. **Risk control capability building**: Build proprietary credit scoring models (like Carvana)
3. **Product innovation**: Consider residual-value guarantee products (like PCP/Japan residual loans)
4. **Compliance first**: Learn from UK FCA transparency requirements, proactively disclose all fees
5. **Channel strategy**: Learn from CarMax's online-offline integration
